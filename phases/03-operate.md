# Phase 3: Operate and Iterate

Entry: verified operational handover and its Director acceptance. Keep START.md's fixed rules active. Load the current atlas context record and verify the active project, phase, baseline, and authority. Do not use an old bootstrap export as current truth.

## Read for the current assignment

Read [CONTEXT_PROTOCOL.md](../CONTEXT_PROTOCOL.md) and the versioned context package supplied by the atlas. Load only the linked topic, proposal, decision, dependency, code, or evidence revisions required for this assignment. The package identifies relevant workflow and acceptance sections; retrieve missing references before dependent work, rather than opening all method files.

The coordinator maintains a brief record of the adopted operational contracts and their source revisions. Workers receive the applicable rules in their packages. A new coordinator loads the workflow and activation contracts relevant to its coordination responsibilities; it must not rely on another agent's unrecorded memory.

## Repeat the operational cycle

1. Record direction or a task/decision nudge in the atlas. Distinguish analysis permission from implementation approval.
2. Prepare or revise a bounded proposal. Obtain any missing Director decision through the atlas.
3. Before dispatch and at consequential work boundaries, check scope, permissions, required context, and current approval-relevant revisions. Persist the exact package supplied to the attempt.
4. Execute authorized work, report actual progress, and verify the specific result against its criteria.
5. Present evidence for review. Record acceptance or rework and update the project record. Assemble a fresh package for the next assignment.

A changed decision triggers impact assessment, not silent mutation of a running attempt. Stop or hold affected work according to the supported execution controls. After interruption, reconcile attempts and side effects before resuming.

For result review, load the approved criteria, exact delivered revision, evidence, known limitations, and relevant decisions. Do not preload unrelated implementation history. Never accept results against superseded criteria without an explicit applicability assessment.

## Change or recovery

This phase is a cycle, not a chain of new instruction files. Material conceptual change uses a versioned proposal and explicit approval in the atlas; load the relevant parts of [Phase 1](01-concept.md) only for that need, without moving authority back into a parallel chat. Required service repair may use scoped build guidance, but it does not erase earlier handover or authorize new scope.

If the service is unavailable, preserve work and record exceptional recovery actions. Reconcile them into the atlas before routine execution resumes. Maintain the phase, active assignment, loaded references, and unresolved blockers for the next session.
